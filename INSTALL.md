# Installing Kharcha (Android sideload)

Kharcha isn't on the Play Store — it's a private app, installed by
downloading an APK directly ("sideloading"). This is completely normal
for a small hobby app and doesn't require anything technical on your
part.

## 1. Download the APK

Open the download link you were sent (a GitHub Release page) on the
Android phone you want to install it on, and tap the `.apk` file to
download it.

## 2. Install it

Tap the downloaded file. Android will show a warning like **"For your
security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this
source."** This is expected — it shows up for any app installed outside
the Play Store, not just this one.

1. Tap **Settings** on that warning.
2. Turn on **"Allow from this source"** for the app you used to download
   it (your browser, or Files/Drive).
3. Go back and tap the APK again → **Install**.

## 3. First launch

1. Open Kharcha.
2. Tap **"Create an account"**, fill in your name/email/password, and
   confirm the email it sends you (check spam if it's slow).
3. Either create your own household, or enter the invite code you were
   given to join someone else's.

## 4. Two settings worth checking

- **Notifications**: allow them when Android asks, so budget alerts and
  the daily reminder actually reach you.
- **Battery optimisation**: Settings → Apps → Kharcha → Battery → set to
  **Unrestricted**. Without this, Android can silently stop the app's
  scheduled reminders from firing.

## Updating later

Kharcha checks for updates itself and shows a banner when a new version
is available — follow the same download-and-install steps above over
the existing install; your account and data are untouched (everything
lives in the cloud, not just on your phone).

## Uninstalling / leaving

Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account or your data — it's
still in the cloud, and you can reinstall and sign back in any time. To
actually delete your account and everything you added, use **Settings →
Delete account** inside the app (see the
[Privacy Policy](https://vineet2102.github.io/Kharcha-App/privacy.html)
for exactly what that does).
