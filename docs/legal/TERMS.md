## What this app is for

owner is not liable for any loss arising from using it, including (but not

household finances.

## Your account

- You are responsible for keeping your password confidential.
- **A household invite code is a credential, not a shareable link.**
  Treat it like a password: don't post it publicly or hand it to anyone
  you don't want to see your household's data. Codes expire and are
  limited to a fixed number of uses, and an admin can revoke one at any
  time.
- You may only join a household you were actually invited to.

## Your data

- Expenses, income, budgets, and recurring rules you add belong to your
  household as a shared record — if you leave or are removed from a
  household, your past entries stay with the household (so the
  household's history stays intact), but your account itself is no longer
  linked to it.
- You can export everything you personally added, and delete your account
  entirely, at any time from inside the app (see the
  [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md) for exactly what deletion does).

## Household administration

An admin of your household can invite and remove members, promote or
demote other admins, and — if they are the sole remaining member — delete
the household outright. The owner of the underlying Supabase project can,
in addition, deactivate or delete any account, for example in response to
abuse or a deletion request.
